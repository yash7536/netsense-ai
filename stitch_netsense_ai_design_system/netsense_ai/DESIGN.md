## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high editorial restraint, structural clarity, and calm authority tailored for enterprise network observability and predictive telemetry in 2026. Rather than relying on generic observability cliches—such as dense dashboard grids, neon wireframes, glow-heavy graphs, or artificial intelligence particle clouds—the interface borrows from the disciplined minimalism and typography-first rigor of high-end editorial publications, mid-century Swiss graphic systems, and precise instrumentation tools.

The target audience comprises Principal Infrastructure Architects, Staff SREs, and Global Network Engineers managing multi-region backbones and edge meshes. The platform delivers instant clarity without sensory overload. Every visual element exists solely to inform or orient. 

### Visual Tenets
- **High Editorial Restraint:** Information density is achieved through whitespace, typographic scale, and structural alignment rather than container boxes, heavy frames, and drop shadows.
- **Anti-Boxy Architecture:** Abandon classic card-bound dashboards. Telemetry, status declarations, and predictive anomalies flow across hairline rules and open asymmetric spatial blocks.
- **Systematic Precision:** Strict dual-typeface pairing cleanly separates editorial narrative and system diagnostics from quantitative telemetric reads.
- **Dignified Restraint:** Zero decorative avatars, zero faux-gradient badges, zero skeuomorphic glows, and zero AI sparkle tropes. Machine intelligence is expressed through confident prose and exact figures.

## Colors

The palette is anchored on `#F7F6F2`, a tailored bone-stone canvas that reduces luminance strain compared to pure sterile white while avoiding the synthetic coldness of typical enterprise greys. 

### Core Palette
- **Canvas Base (`#F7F6F2`):** Warm, refined stone/bone base tone for full viewport backgrounds and baseline surface planes.
- **Pure Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved strictly for interactive inputs, selectable data rows upon state focus, and intentional raised foreground panes.
- **Primary Ink (`#111111`):** Deep charcoal black for display headers, primary telemetry metrics, and dominant editorial statements.
- **Secondary Graphite (`#666666`):** Balanced neutral grey for descriptive copy, column headers, axis indices, and inline contextual captions.
- **Muted Stone (`#999990`):** Soft tertiary value used exclusively for disabled states, non-actionable sub-indicators, and structural reference markers.
- **Hairline Border (`#E3E1DC`):** Razor-thin structural perimeter and divider line tone that harmonizes precisely with the bone canvas.
- **Subtle Surface Tint (`#EFECE6`):** Very light surface contrast tone used for table header bars, neutral hover states, and inactive tab tracks.

### Semantic & Status Accents
- **Accent Royal Cobalt (`#3157D5`):** Punctual, razor-sharp blue reserved strictly for deliberate focal points: active interactive targets, predictive anomaly cursors, and primary operational triggers.
- **Success Sage (`#3D7A55`):** Understated, deep forest sage conveying operational stability and verified throughput without screaming for attention.
- **Warning Amber (`#B7791F`):** Muted ochre amber indicating degradation, latency drifts, or predictive threshold proximity.
- **Critical Crimson (`#C74646`):** Refined terracotta crimson alerting to packet drop spikes, route blackholes, or interface drops without fluorescent hostility.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements an unwavering protocol between prose and telemetry:

1. **IBM Plex Sans** governs all human-facing expressions: platform headlines, system status statements, explanatory narratives, structural navigational labels, field descriptions, and analytical summaries. It conveys disciplined intelligence and journalistic objectivity.
2. **IBM Plex Mono** is employed strictly for verifiable technical telemetry values: IPv4/IPv6 addresses, BGP Autonomous System Numbers (ASNs), nanosecond/millisecond latencies, packet drop ratios, predictive anomaly confidence scores, interface IDs, epoch timestamps, and raw payloads. It must never be applied to prose or regular paragraph labels.

### Rules of Usage
- Never use full uppercase styling for editorial sentences. 
- Uppercase styling is permitted only for `editorial-caption` tracker headers and specific technical acronyms (e.g., `RTT`, `TTL`, `BGP`, `P99`).
- Numbers embedded inside human prose (e.g., "Observed 3 anomalies across US-East") must remain in IBM Plex Sans. Mono is strictly reserved for measured instrument telemetry (e.g., `34.2 ms`, `0.002%`, `10.240.12.1`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural grid with deliberate, asymmetrical editorial divisions. Rather than clustering data into discrete boxes or cards, information spans broad open canvases defined by vertical and horizontal hairlines.

### Layout Model
- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column variable fluid grid framed by a fixed minimum gutter of `1.5rem` (24px) up to `2rem` (32px) on large screens, maxed out at `1680px` centered width with edge bleed margins.
- **Asymmetric Anchoring:** High-level narrative overviews and predictive system health occupy 4 or 5 columns on the left, while dense, continuous telemetry logs, timeseries, and route matrices unfold across the remaining columns.
- **Inline Telemetry:** Metrics are rendered directly against the canvas surface. Numerical indicators sit beneath hairline rules rather than inside enclosed container cells.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous macro spacing (`space-3xl` and `space-4xl`) creates pause and focus between system domains (e.g., Global Ingress vs. Core Mesh), while micro components maintain compact, dense operational spacing (`space-xs` to `space-md`).

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Flow
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Full asymmetric 12-column layouts with multi-pane inspection. Left-aligned master diagnostics with right-aligned drill-downs.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** Reflows to an 8-column layout. Split panes stack into sequential diagnostic ribbons separated by full-width 1px hairline rules.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column stacked continuous flow with `1rem` horizontal padding. Complex multi-column data tables collapse to horizontally scrollable hairline telemetry strips.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately eschews standard drop shadows, diffused blurred backdrops, glowing borders, and pseudo-3D layers. Depth is achieved strictly through planar contrast, hairline delineation, and subtle tonal layering.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Hairline Framing:** All visual containment uses crisp 1px borders colored `#E3E1DC`. Borders never exceed 1px.
- **Zero Drop Shadow:** Components, toolbars, dropdowns, and flyout diagnostic panels carry `box-shadow: none`. 
- **Surface Elevation via Tonal Contrast:**
  - *Base Ground (Level 0):* Canvas `#F7F6F2`.
  - *Active / Focus Ground (Level 1):* `#FFFFFF` flat fills with `#E3E1DC` border for floating menus, dropdown overlays, and contextual inspector trays.
  - *Tonal Inset (Level -1):* `#EFECE6` flat fill with no border for passive timeline backgrounds and table column category markers.
- **Overlay Hierarchy:** Flyouts and modal command palettes open without darkening backdrop filters. Instead, a clean 1px hairline divider pins the inspector drawer to the canvas margin, maintaining structural alignment with the rest of the interface.

## Shapes

The geometric identity is uncompromisingly sharp (`roundedness: 0`). Corner radii across all structural and interactive elements are strictly `0px` or capped at a micro radius of `2px` exclusively for form inputs and discrete action triggers to prevent visual aliasing on subpixel displays.

### Form Characteristics
- **Cards, Panes, Modals:** Rigid `0px` rectangular corners. Sharp intersections emphasize the precision instrumentation of network infrastructure.
- **Buttons, Toggles, Form Fields:** `0px` sharp corners with `1px` crisp borders.
- **Data Points & Sparkline Nodes:** Diamond or crisp rectangular markers (`3px` x `3px`) in place of circular dots.
- **Status Indicators:** Micro rectangles (`6px` width x `2px` height) or precise vertical bars rather than circular status orbs.

## Components

### Buttons & Triggers
- **Primary Button:** Solid `#111111` background, `#F7F6F2` text, 0px radius, 1px `#111111` border. Active state shifts background to `#3157D5` with immediate 100ms transition. Padding: `0.5rem 1.25rem`. Typography: `body-md` in medium weight.
- **Secondary Button:** Transparent background, `#111111` text, 1px `#E3E1DC` border. Hover changes border to `#111111`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Trigger:** Transparent background, `#666666` text, no border. Hover shifts text to `#111111` with an underline positioned 4px beneath the baseline.
- **Destructive Action:** Transparent background, `#C74646` text, 1px border colored `#C74646`. Hover state fills with `#C74646` and white text.

### Telemetry & KPI Displays (Anti-Boxy)
- Replace enclosed card boxes with an open hairline format: a continuous horizontal 1px `#E3E1DC` border on top, followed by an `editorial-caption` title in `#666666` (IBM Plex Sans), followed by a massive `telemetry-hero` value in `#111111` (IBM Plex Mono).
- Secondary deltas sit inline immediately below the value in `telemetry-sm` (IBM Plex Mono), prefixed with directional glyphs (`+`, `-`) using `#3D7A55` (improvement) or `#C74646` (degradation).

### Tables & Data Grids
- **Container:** Borderless outer grid resting directly on canvas `#F7F6F2`.
- **Header Row:** Height `36px`, border-bottom 1px `#111111` (anchor rule). Header text uses `editorial-caption` in uppercase `#666666`.
- **Data Rows:** Height `44px`, horizontal border-bottom 1px `#E3E1DC`, no vertical column dividers. Background is transparent on rest; turns `#FFFFFF` on hover with a 1px solid `#3157D5` accent line on the extreme left edge (2px width).
- **Column Typography:** Narrative entities (Hostname, Region, Routing Protocol) use IBM Plex Sans (`body-md`). Telemetric metrics (RTT, Loss %, BGP State, IP Prefix) use IBM Plex Mono (`telemetry-md`) aligned strictly right.

### Input Fields & Search
- Flat `#FFFFFF` or transparent base with a 1px border in `#E3E1DC`. No internal shadow or rounded corners.
- Padding: `0.625rem 0.75rem`.
- Font: IBM Plex Sans for general query search; IBM Plex Mono automatically activates when the input detects CIDR blocks, IPs, or regex syntax.
- Focus State: Border color switches immediately to `#111111` with no focus ring blur.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkbox:** Square `14px` x `14px`, 0px border-radius, 1px border `#666666`. Checked state fills `#111111` with an interior pure white sharp micro check.
- **Radio Button:** Square `14px` x `14px` exterior, 0px radius, 1px border `#666666`. Selected state reveals an inner solid `#111111` square of `6px` x `6px`.

### Status Indicators & Badges
- Avoid rounded pill badges and colored background chips.
- Status is conveyed through an inline micro-dash (`6px` x `2px`) followed by the status descriptor in uppercase IBM Plex Sans (`editorial-caption`).
- Color mappings: Nominal uses `#3D7A55`, Predictive Drift uses `#B7791F`, Route Degradation uses `#C74646`, Inactive uses `#999990`.

### Predictive Anomaly Sparklines & Streams
- Rendered with 1.25px vector strokes without area gradient fills below the curve.
- Baseline history is drawn in `#666666`.
- Predictive horizons are delineated by a 1px vertical hairline rule in `#3157D5` followed by a dotted stroke projected into future timestamp ranges.
